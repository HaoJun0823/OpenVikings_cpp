# AI collection flags and warehouse carriers (RM034)

On October 17, 2026 the user authorized changing the remaster AI so collectors can harvest at resource deposits, leave goods near their work flags and use warehouse carriers for delivery. This intentionally changes Windows behavior on `remaster`; the frozen `reversed-game-authentic-behaviour` branch and historical original-behavior register are preserved.

## Original behavior and observed stalled economy

The inspected `Militär: COOP` autosaves contain an enabled red AI with 22 humans, six completed buildings and no active construction. Ordinary human records and positions continue changing, so these snapshots do not show a frozen simulation. The resource manager already knows stone at `(107, 376)` within the village centered on `(91, 351)`, radius 50. The assigned collector's work center remains `(91, 351)` while the warehouse's stone stock is zero. Stock arrays are indexed by the house's configured stock slots, not by good ID; decoding the installed `housetypes.cif` confirms that wood, clay, flour and bricks are stocked, rather than empty.

Windows `0045778B` configures a producer's job, workplace and product, then returns whether its warehouse already contains the product. At `00457838` it calls the stock getter `00431944`, and `0045783F` branches to failure on zero. Collection entry `004578A3` calls that predicate at `004578B6`; `004578BD` skips its work-center lookup when false. Thus an empty stock can prevent an otherwise configured collector from receiving the known resource position. This condition is original behavior, not proof of a reconstruction error.

Native collector and carrier execution already support the requested separation. Unattached collectors use the existing map-pile placement path (`00474F17`) near their work center. A carrier attached to a warehouse searches for demanded goods around its own work center (`00460327`, radius 20 for carriers), including landscape piles, and returns them through the normal stock/reservation flow. Native commands are opcode-indexed slots (`0043BB4E`), with priority selected by slot order (`00434F03`); issuing commands in a particular insertion order does not create a FIFO.

## Remaster policy

- The new branch applies to map-resource requirements with type 1 and collector job 8. Farmers, hunters, fishers, workshop workers, military tasks and human-player control retain their existing execution paths.
- The producer's existing house ID/revision now identifies a finished delivery warehouse; a collector slot in that house is no longer required. Existing resource/village searches and bounds remain in effect. This does not create unlimited-range resource knowledge or teleport goods.
- The AI independently configures the collector's job, detaches its workplace, selects its allowed product and moves its work flag to the known deposit. Each assignment waits for pending work commands before the next step. It does not wait for a positive stock before updating the flag.
- Collectors use unchanged harvest, carry, pile-placement, pathfinding and need-handling routines. The existing enemy-near-resource check prevents new field operation at a threatened site.
- A full destination pauses the collector by queuing native production command 23 with a counter reduction of 11, the existing infinite-counter value. Selecting product zero is not used to pause: the current capability predicate rejects zero. Collection resumes when destination capacity is available. Full stocks without an existing producer do not reserve another idle worker.
- Carriers retain a normal warehouse attachment and receive a pickup work center at the collector's flag. Existing carriers at that flag are reused. Empty carriers whose previous site is no longer needed can be reassigned while idle or working; the native work-center command preserves their current pickup/delivery target and reservations. Needs tasks are left alone. Otherwise the AI prefers an unattached qualified carrier, or recruits a qualified free civilian/builder; active construction, road/wall work, cargo, existing AI reservations and pending commands exclude candidates. The existing warehouse carrier-slot limit is respected.
- Job-change 21 and warehouse attachment 28 are queued first. The pickup-center command 17 is issued only after attachment is observed, because opcode order would otherwise execute 17 too early and attachment would reset the center to the warehouse door.
- Warehouse carriers and pending carrier assignments are reserved against other high-level AI allocations. Both passes of the single-player worker-request search also exclude these carriers and detached collectors still owned by a village producer record. When collection is retired, the collector is returned to the available civilian job through the existing command path, including replacement of a pending collector job change. Other producer cleanup retains its original path.
- Supply confirmation still observes goods actually delivered to the destination warehouse. Starting a detached collector alone does not declare an empty warehouse stocked.

All persistence uses existing producer references, human jobs, production counters, workplace references, work centers and command slots. No serialized record is enlarged and no new transient route table is needed after save/load. The normal native delivery reservations, allocation families and ownership are unchanged. Existing field piles remain available for collection. If no suitable carrier or warehouse slot is available, delivery must wait; no units, stock, permissions or extra worker slots are fabricated.

Project/filter registration and physical storage models are unchanged. No tests, Debug/Release build or game execution was performed.
